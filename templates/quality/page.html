On the Operator Kiosk, Roll tags › Take a picture of the tag reads the tag on the tablet (nothing leaves the plant network) and fills in the fields. Each field is found by the labels printed next to it on your suppliers' tags, with the value after the label or on the line under it. Add the words your tags use. A pattern (a regular expression) finds a value that has no label, e.g. R\d{6}. GS1 barcodes fill the roll (21) and lot (10) themselves; a plain barcode fills the field picked below.
