Turning the raw reading into real units

  • Two-point scaling: raw low / high → value low / high (0–5 V = 0–500 mm). Ready-made raw ranges: {% for k, l, lo, hi, u in presets %}{{ l }}{% if not forloop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}.
  • Reversed scales work too (0 V = 500 mm, 5 V = 0 mm, for a sensor that reads distance to a shrinking roll).
  • Keep inside the range or let it run past the ends · calibration offset · decimals · average of the last N readings.
  • Fault limits on the raw reading (below 3.6 mA = broken wire) mark the sensor faulty.
  • On / off sensors: your own words for on and off, and active-low inputs.
  • Formulas across sensors (average of two, a difference, a ratio).

What an alarm can do

  • Show in the machine's kiosk Alarms with the badge, or on every machine's kiosk
  • Stay until someone acknowledges it (who and when is logged)
  • Email or text people from Admin › Alerts: everyone for that machine or the ones you pick, after N minutes, repeating every N minutes, and when it clears
  • Open a maintenance request with its type and priority
  • Go to the webhook as sensor_alarm / sensor_alarm_clear
  • Kept in the alarm log, with the value when it started and the worst while it lasted; minute values in sensor_values for Grafana
