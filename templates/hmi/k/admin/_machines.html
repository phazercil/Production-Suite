Rated speed sets the default feed rate and the dashed line on the speed chart. The speed unit is what the machine reports: pleats per minute, linear mm per second (one part = its mm of media: pleats × 2 × pleat height, or the mm per part you set) or parts per minute. Counts without a counter, run rates and the Scheduler follow it. Taking a machine out of service moves its queued MOs back to Unscheduled and hides it from Start Shift.
