The machine schedule: when each shift runs, on which days, its lunch, breaks and authorized downtimes (e.g. start-up at the start of the shift, or shutting the machines down 15 minutes before the end to clean them out). A stop during any of these isn't downtime, and the schedule's run times pause over them. Time outside every shift isn't downtime either. An end time earlier than the start runs past midnight. Seasonal variants change a shift for a range of dates (e.g. an extra hour in July) without touching its main schedule. A change counts from today on: the days before keep the schedule they ran.
