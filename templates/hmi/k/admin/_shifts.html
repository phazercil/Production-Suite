The machine schedule: when each shift runs, its lunch, breaks and authorized downtimes (e.g. start-up at the start of the shift, or shutting the machines down 15 minutes before the end to clean them out). A stop during any of these isn't downtime, and the schedule's run times pause over them. Time outside every shift isn't downtime either. An end time earlier than the start runs past midnight. The Labor Budget follows these shifts.
