Grafana queries that match the suite

The suite leaves planned time out of downtime: Scheduler breaks and lunches, and {{ S.rules.startGrace|default:0 }} minutes of start-up after each shift's scheduled start. It marks those minutes so Grafana can do the same: hmi_ppm.planned is 1 for a minute inside planned time and hmi_ppm.run is 1 for a running minute, hmi_planned lists each planned stretch per line and shift, and hmi_downtime never includes planned time. Paste these into your panels (MySQL data source on the suite's database, with a line dashboard variable).
